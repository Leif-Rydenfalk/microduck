GripBAT 2S is featured as 3500mAh/26Wh high capacity that is able to bear high current work. Compatible with NP-F550/F570, the GripBAT 2S can be widely used with accessories such as wireless video transmitter, monitor, wireless lens control in the film industry. Moreover, its capacity is 75% higher than regular 2000mAh batteries in the market, strong power with long endurance.

Besides the super high capacity 3500mA, the battery cell and built-in PCB give GripBAT 2S the ability to support high current and high load output, that means it supports continuous output current up to 5A and continuous output power up to 40W to meet harsh environment. Not limited to these, built-in PCB not only adds protections to regular overvoltage, undervoltage, overcurrent and over-temperature, but has the power to output continuous high current and protect higher overcurrent.

Extensive Applications

Benefiting from the broad use of NP-F series batteries in the film industry, such as SmallHD monitor, Atomos monitor, Movcam Wireless Lens Control, Wireless Video Transmitter and Receiver, and film lights from Aputure, Nanguang, etc, as well as the NBox livestream box and so on, the GripBAT 2S with super high capacity is sure to work with above products. With the advantage of 75% higher than regular NP-F550 in capacity, the GripBAT 2S is portable to use with enough electricity though its volume is a fraction of NP-F750!

The GripBAT 2S also serves as an UPS power source for MAVO Edge 8K: The creative power solution can not only power the MAVO Edge independently, allowing for an efficient kit space utilization, but also work as UPS power source to supply uninterrupted power to the camera when replacing V-mount batteries or BP-U30 type batteries.

Red and White Color Design

Though the GripBAT 2S is compatible with SONY NP-F550/F570 battery, Kinefinity makes a creative design to the appearance of GripBAT 2S. The red color and white color response with each other, which is feel of energy and has a distinguished recognition.

With Kinefinity whole-new PD Hybrid Dual Charger, charging time for one GripBAT 2S is only 60 min and for two GripBAT 2S is only 90 min.
