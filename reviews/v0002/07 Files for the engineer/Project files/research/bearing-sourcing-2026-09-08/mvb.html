ET-2216

ThinSectionDGBB (Thin Section Deep Groove Ball Bearings) are characterized by their narrow and lightweight design. They feature a thin profile, which allows for more design flexibility and running precision compared to standard bearings. These bearings have deep raceways for steel balls and a cage. Their compact structure saves space. They are often used in highly technical applications such as aerospace assemblies, medical systems, and robotics, where space and weight constraints exist, and high accuracy, low friction, and reliable operation are required.

Features：

Compact and lightweight design, saving significant space.
High running precision, ensuring smooth and accurate rotation.
Suitable for applications with strict space and weight limitations, providing reliable performance.
