7 · Beside the real product

The only published asset of the product performing this motion is Pollen's portrait move clip images/moves-portrait-alpha_walk.webm (400 frames, 50 fps, 8.000 s, 800×1280, vp9). It is Pollen's own rendered clip, not a photograph — no photograph of the product mid-stride exists in images/, so this is a render-to-render comparison and is labelled as one.

Both gait periods are measured, not assumed. The product's comes off the pixels: the decoded background is exactly black (938 762 of 1 024 000 px at luma 0 on frame 100), so the silhouette is luma > 0.5 plus any 0-luma pixel enclosed by silhouette on all four sides of its row and column — otherwise the robot's own black plastic is knocked out. Then the x-centroid of the bottom 15 % of the silhouette minus the x-centroid of the whole silhouette — one oscillation per gait cycle — autocorrelated. Ours comes off left_knee in the trajectory. Both signals are sampled at 50 Hz, so each period is quantised to 0.020 s: the two agree to within one sample, which is the resolution of the measurement and not a claim of exact equality.
