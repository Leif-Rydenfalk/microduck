# part:jst-seh-001t-p0.6 — JST SEH-001T-P0.6 EH crimp contact

Three per EHR-3 housing. Wire window AWG #30–#22 (0.05–0.33 mm²), insulation
O.D. 1.0–1.9 mm (`docs/fetched/eEH.pdf` p.2). Phosphor bronze, tin plated.

The finding this folder exists to carry: ROBOTIS states "Wire Gauge for
DYNAMIXEL 21 AWG" next to this contact number; 21 AWG is 0.4105 mm², 24 %
over the contact's published maximum. CANNOT DETERMINE which document is
right until a lead is stripped and measured — `evidence/ledger.jsonl`.

The solid is an envelope read off a drawing whose leaders pdftotext lost;
the reading is stated in `cad/part.py`. Do not use it for a fit check.
