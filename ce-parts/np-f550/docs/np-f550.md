# NP-F550 — the Microduck's removable 2S Li-ion pack (form factor known, fitted pack not)

*Press kit: removable NP-F550, 2600 mAh, ~1 h. Runtime window 6.6–8.2 V under
load through the servos (`model.rs:99-128`); "no fuel gauge and no ADC"
(`docs/ELECTRONICS-AND-SOFTWARE.md` §9).*

## Identity

"NP-F550" is Sony's original InfoLITHIUM L-series pack — 1500 mAh / 10.8 Wh.
A **2600 mAh** NP-F550 is therefore a third-party pack in Sony's shape, and no
Pollen source names the maker. Verdict: **CANNOT DETERMINE** (the label on a
shipped pack settles it).

## Contact pinout — CANNOT DETERMINE

No vendor document for the NP-F contact face was found; every hit was a
forum thread or a dummy-battery listing without a drawing. The community
reading (+, −, a "C" data/thermistor contact, sometimes "T") is **not**
recorded as fact. The robot needs only + and −; the banana contact PCB's
pitch is the open geometric question. Settles it: a photo + ohmmeter on a
physical pack (origin `measured`), or a plate vendor's dimensioned drawing.

## 2S references on this shelf

- protection: `part:s-8252` (ABLIC, 2-serial-cell protection IC)
- charger: `part:mcp73213` (Microchip, 2S linear charger with OVP; needs ≥ ~8.7 V in — 5 V USB cannot charge this pack through it)

Standards: UN 38.3 (transport) and IEC 62133-2 (safety) govern Li-ion packs in
general; neither fetched page names them for its pack, so no `standard`
ledger row is written.
