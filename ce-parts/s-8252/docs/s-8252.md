What the sheet says, verbatim (whitespace collapsed):

- `Input voltage between VDD pin and VSS pin VDS VDD VSS − 0.3 to VSS + 12 V` (Table 8, absolute maximum)
- `VM pin input voltage VVM VM VDD − 28 to VDD + 0.3 V` (Table 8)
- `Operation voltage between VDD pin and VSS pin VDSOP1 − 1.5 − 10 V −` (Electrical Characteristics)
- `Current consumption during operation IOPE V1 = V2 = 3.5 V, V3 = 0 V − 4.0 8.0 μA 2`
- `Overcharge detection voltage n (n = 1, 2) 3.550 V to 4.600 V (5 mV steps) Accuracy ±20 mV (Ta = +25°C)` (Features)

Pins, SOT-23-6 (Table 6): 1 DO · 2 CO · 3 VM · 4 VC · 5 VDD · 6 VSS.

Not in the sheet, so not here: the ampere overcurrent threshold (a VM-pin
voltage over the external FETs' RDS(on)); which variant a Microduck rebuild
should fit (depends on the pack's cell, itself unidentified — `part:np-f550`).

Standard: none governs a protection IC's thresholds. The only standard the
sheet names is JEDEC 51-7 for the power-dissipation test board — not a
governing standard, so no `standard` ledger row is written.
