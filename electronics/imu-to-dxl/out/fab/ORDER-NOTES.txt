Microduck imu_to_dxl v2 (reconstruction)
========================================

These are the board author's own notes, shipped WITH the Gerbers
because the fab README does not carry them. Read them before
ordering; several are the reason a number is what it is.

DRC verdict: FAIL

1. _resolve_offboard(): 118 netlist terminals resolved as not-pads-of-this-board. The robot netlist is the WHOLE robot; this board is one device on it. First ten reasons:
  - host.5V (net V5_HAT): off-board — net V5_HAT has no pad on the imu_to_dxl board
  - hat.V5_OUT (net V5_HAT): off-board — net V5_HAT has no pad on the imu_to_dxl board
  - host.GND (net GND): another device on the same bus node — its terminal lands on this board's own pads on that net (J1.1)
  - battery.BAT- (net GND): another device on the same bus node — its terminal lands on this board's own pads on that net (J1.1)
  - hat.GND (net GND): another device on the same bus node — its terminal lands on this board's own pads on that net (J1.1)
  - codec.GND (net GND): another device on the same bus node — its terminal lands on this board's own pads on that net (J1.1)
  - bmi088.GND (net GND): another device on the same bus node — its terminal lands on this board's own pads on that net (J1.1)
  - tof.GND (net GND): another device on the same bus node — its terminal lands on this board's own pads on that net (J1.1)
  - id20.GND (net GND): another device on the same bus node — its terminal lands on this board's own pads on that net (J1.1)
  - id21.GND (net GND): another device on the same bus node — its terminal lands on this board's own pads on that net (J1.1)

2. THE `binding` CHECK REPORTS CANNOT DETERMINE, AND THAT IS CORRECT. Fourteen nets exist on this board and not in the robot netlist — BYPASS, DIR, DXL_DRV, IMU_INT1, MCU_RX, MCU_TX, NRST, SPI_MISO, SPI_MOSI, SPI_NSS, SPI_SCK, SWCLK, SWDIO, V3V3. They are board-LOCAL and they must be: electronics/netlist.py models this whole board as ONE device, `imu200`, with exactly three terminals (GND, VDD, DATA — 'rides the Dynamixel bus'), because that is all Pollen's published source says about it. Everything inside the box is this file's own circuit. The check is right to say it cannot verify what the netlist does not model, and it would be wrong to silence it.

3. WHERE THIS BOARD SITS, MEASURED 2026-09-02. The MJCF puts the `imu` SITE on trunk_base at body (-21.000, 0.100, -14.700) mm. Every mesh placed in that same body was transformed into the body frame and the site's clearance to each bounding box computed:
  trunk_base plate .... 16.600 mm clear in y (the site is inside its x and z ranges)
  power_support ....... 15.179 mm clear in z
  np_f970 (the pack) .. 0.524 mm clear in z, and INSIDE its x and y ranges — the site is 0.524 mm BELOW the pack's bounding box
  banana_pcb_locker ... 49.074 mm clear in y
  xl330 (both hip yaw servos) ... 12.500 mm clear in x
THE PACK IS THE CONSTRAINT. 0.524 mm to the battery means this board lies FLAT under the pack, in the body x-y plane, and its component side must face AWAY from the pack (-z). Every part on this board is on the top layer, so the board is fitted component-side-down; nothing here may be taller than the clearance BELOW it, which is a number the shells decide and this lane has not measured. The tallest part is the JST EH housing at 6.500 mm.
This is a BOUND, not a clearance study: it uses bounding boxes, not solids, and it does not include left_shell or right_shell.

4. OUTLINE IS NOT MEASURED. 40.000 x 22.000 mm r2.0 with two M2 holes at (3.000, 18.500) and (37.000, 18.500) is decision E8. Pollen ships no mesh, no drawing and no photograph of this board; the MJCF places only an `imu` SITE at trunk_base body (-21, 0.1, -14.7) -> world (-21, 0, 105.3) mm (docs/ELECTRONICS-AND-SOFTWARE.md §4.1). A clearance check of this rectangle against trunk_base is a mechanical task, not a PCB one, and it has not been run.

5. E10 — THE POWER PATH IS A PASS-THROUGH. SERVO_V arrives on J1 and leaves on J2 and J3, so this board's copper carries every device downstream of it: with wiring/CABLES.md's harness that is both leg chains, ten XL330 servos. It is hand-laid at 2.000 mm on F.Cu AND mirrored on B.Cu with four stitching vias, claimed before the router runs. IPC-2221 §6.2 gives 3.95 A per layer at a 10 degC rise (5.36 A at 20 degC); whether the pair carries twice that is NOT something IPC-2221 answers — the two runs are 1.6 mm apart in one thermal mass — so the honest figure is the per-layer one. The first build of this board let the autorouter draw this net at 0.400 mm, which the same equation puts at 1.23 A. GND returns through the B.Cu pour, which is the width of the board.

6. CURRENT: no net on this board has a documented total. The published figures are the chip's alone — 'IddHP | Gyroscope and accelerometer current consumption in high-performance mode | 0.65 | mA' (DS13510 Table 4 p.14/198) — and the record says so explicitly: 'The board's MCU and transceiver draw are unpublished, so the board total is CANNOT DETERMINE; this figure is a floor, not the board' (ce-parts/microduck-imu-to-dxl/electrical.part.json). check_current therefore reports CANNOT DETERMINE and no track is sized from a guess.
