Microduck banana battery-contact PCB (reconstruction)
=====================================================

These are the board author's own notes, shipped WITH the Gerbers
because the fab README does not carry them. Read them before
ordering; several are the reason a number is what it is.

DRC verdict: CANNOT DETERMINE

1. _resolve_offboard(): 119 netlist terminals resolved as not-pads-of-this-board. The netlist is the WHOLE robot; this board is a 45.800 x 5.100 mm contact strip.

2. THE `binding` CHECK REPORTS CANNOT DETERMINE, AND THAT IS CORRECT. BAT_T exists on this board and not in the robot netlist, because electronics/netlist.py wires the pack as {BAT+: VBAT, BAT-: GND} and nothing in Pollen's published source reads the pack's third terminal. Decision B6 fits it anyway. The check is right to say it cannot verify a net the netlist does not model.

3. POCKET, MEASURED 2026-09-02 off Pollen's own meshes by RAY-CASTING the power_support solid at y = 31.000 (mid-gap), not by reading section outlines: clear width 46.292 mm between the screw posts' inner faces (x -23.146..+23.146), clear height 4.696 mm from the rib top z 50.930 to the locker bar's top z 55.626, clear depth 8.000 mm (y 27.000 plate face .. 35.000 locker bar). This board is 45.800 x 4.600 x 1.6 mm, so it enters with 0.246 mm per side in x and 0.048 mm per side in z. The first cut of this board was 5.100 mm tall because it used the head plate's top edge as the ceiling; the plate is BEHIND the board, not above it.

4. CONTACT PITCH IS DERIVED, NOT MEASURED. 4.000 mm = the 12.000 mm window of the power_support head plate divided into three cells. Sony publishes no NP-F terminal drawing; a caliper across a physical NP-F550 pack settles it and nothing else does. Every document that repeats this number must repeat that sentence.

5. COPPER CAPACITY: the two power tracks are 1.500 mm wide on 1 oz outer copper. IPC-2221 §6.2 I = 0.048 * dT^0.44 * A^0.725 (A in mil^2) gives 3.21 A at a 10 degC rise (4.35 A at 20 degC). The DEMAND is CANNOT DETERMINE: wiring/CABLES.md's own worst case is an assumed 1 A per moving servo (the vendor publishes only 17 mA standby and 1.47 A stall at 5 V) and Sony publishes no maximum continuous discharge current for the pack.

6. SURFACE FINISH: ENIG, not the HASL default. A sliding spring contact on a solder-coated pad cold-flows and oxidises. JLCPCB publishes 'HASL (leaded / lead-free), ENIG, OSP' for this process (jlcpcb.com/capabilities/pcb-capabilities, re-read 2026-09-02), so this is an order option, not a special process. THE GERBERS DO NOT CARRY THE FINISH — it is an order-form field, and it is in the BOM and in the fab README because of that.
