Walking — not all PASS. Every solved part carries the measured walking peak; the governing part is part:microduck-motor-support at SF 1.411 (peak von Mises 35.427 MPa against the class-table yield 50 MPa, 1.440 against the fetched TDS yield, 0.480 against the 17 MPa interlayer figure). The walking load is what MuJoCo measured, not a round number: the body forces at the gait peak, expressed in each part's own frame (§2).
A 0.250 m fall — FAIL. The governing part under the landing load is part:microduck-motor-support (drop case) at SF 0.273: peak von Mises 182.891 MPa, linear failure load 8.98 N against the 32.860 N applied. The applied drop force is MuJoCo's default-contact peak, which §6 shows is the soft end of a bracket that reaches 779 N (Hertz heel arc, TDS modulus) to 10214 N (rigid on the sole floor stiffness). Parts that fail at the soft end fail at every point of the bracket.
FAIL list. part:microduck-foot-left drop — SF 1.086 (peak 46.0 MPa leaves SF 1.09 against 50 MPa yield; the case requires 2); part:microduck-shin drop — SF 0.495 (peak 101.0 MPa leaves SF 0.50 against 50 MPa yield; the case requires 2); part:microduck-hip-bracket drop — SF 0.631 (peak 79.2 MPa leaves SF 0.63 against 50 MPa yield; the case requires 2); part:microduck-yaw2roll drop — SF 1.766 (peak 28.3 MPa leaves SF 1.77 against 50 MPa yield; the case requires 2); part:microduck-neck-plate drop — SF 1.254 (peak 39.9 MPa leaves SF 1.25 against 50 MPa yield; the case requires 2); part:microduck-motor-support walk — SF 1.411 (peak 35.4 MPa leaves SF 1.41 against 50 MPa yield; the case requires 2); part:microduck-motor-support drop — SF 0.273 (peak 182.9 MPa leaves SF 0.27 against 50 MPa yield; the case requires 2); part:microduck-power-support drop — SF 1.652 (peak 30.3 MPa leaves SF 1.65 against 50 MPa yield; the case requires 2).
Buckling — CANNOT DETERMINE/FAIL/PASS. microduck-neck-plate: buckling load factor 4.8026 on the 24.4404 N landing load (critical 117.38 N); ce-struct rule factor >= 2; higher factors [38.8937, 84.4385]. microduck-shin: buckling load factor 0.9262 on the 118.8160 N landing load (critical 110.05 N); ce-struct rule factor >= 2; higher factors [6.6641, 8.1911]. microduck-upper-leg-rigidity-plate: the three lowest eigen-factors [1.000246, 1.000266, 1.001148] lie within 1 % of each other — a localised mode at the loaded end face (voxel edge crushing), not a plate mo.
Fatigue on the ankle — PASS. peak walk-cycle von Mises 1.275 MPa on the ankle (walk-peak force 22.0262 N) is 29.9 % of the P_s>=90 % design endurance limit 4.26 MPa (0.1 x UTS 42.6 MPa, Ezeh & Susmel 2019) even on the least favourable basis — infinite life by the design curve at 22461 cycles/km (period 0.405 s, stride 0.0445 m)
Mesh convergence (ankle, drop) — PASS. peak von Mises moved 2.17 % between the two finest meshes (< 10 %); deflection moved 6.49 %
Thigh housing — CANNOT DETERMINE. no tetrahedral mesh of the thigh housing exists: 6 mesh sizes x 12 mesher strategies all refuse on the same sliver at the R12.2-arc / side-wall tangency (x 66.6, y -12.2, z 0); the solid is valid to OCC but its every tessellation self-intersects there. The thigh's load case (walk 19.064 N, drop 109.556 N at the knee, loads_mujoco.json) is declared and waits on that one junction. Bounding evidence in the meantime: the 1 mm rigidity plate that closes the same cup is solved with 100 % of the thigh load (fea_microduck-upper-leg-rigidity-plate_*.json).

10Method and honest limits

  • Loads. sim/measure_loads.py: stock physics of Pollen's MJCF (our meshes swap only the visuals; run_policy.py measured stock-vs-ours qpos identical), policy replayed at 50 Hz, contact forces and body interaction forces sampled at every 0.005 s physics step; drops at 0.005 s and 50 µs steps.
  • FEA. sim/stress_all.py over cecad.stress: gmsh C3D10 at a wall-aware size (clamp(thinnest bbox side / 3, 0.8, 1.5) mm — the plate heuristic alone put 8.5 mm elements on the ankle's 2.5 mm walls), CalculiX static, nodal von Mises peak. A peak at a re-entrant corner rises with refinement; §5 measures that on the governing case.
  • Patches. Held and loaded regions are boundary faces near the named connector (whole feature for axial kinds, four element faces otherwise) — cecad records each as an assumption and it is quoted under every figure. A connector that sits on an axis loads the nearest skin, not a contact that was measured.
  • Pictures. cecad.feaimage renders the .frd the verdict came from and refuses if the recomputed peak differs from the report by more than 0.5 %; every PNG is read back (size, colours, foreground fraction) and the facts are printed under it.
  • Buckling / modal. sim/struct_ce.py through ce-struct (:8099): voxel hex mesh at 0.33–1.0 mm cells, CalculiX *BUCKLE (3 modes) and *FREQUENCY, on the three slender members; the load enters over an end face, which is an idealisation of screws.
  • Not done. No physical part has been loaded. No contact/nonlinear solve. No two-body (assembly) solve — the trunk base and the rigidity plate need one. No thermal coupling (lane F3).
