The system does not close, and the place it fails is the one that carries all the power. The fifteen XL330 servos sit on +BATT with nothing between them and the pack: on Pollen’s own board file the pads J13.2, J14.2, J3.2 and J11.2 are all +BATT, and no regulator on the board has +BATT on an output. The pack runs 6.6–8.2 V under load; the servo is rated 3.7–6.0 V. That is 10.0 % over its maximum when the pack is empty and 36.7 % over when it is full, with no operating point anywhere in the pack’s range where the actuators are in specification. Pollen do not fix it — they write Shutdown(63) = 52 into every servo at boot, which is the vendor default 53 with bit 0, Input Voltage Error, cleared.
