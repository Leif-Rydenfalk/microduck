Nano/Orin NX SKU B0183 Be the first to review this product Special Price $23.99 was $39.99 Qty - + Add to Cart Wish List Compare Details Item Description This Arducam IMX219 camera module is mainly designed for NVIDIA®Jetson Board. The highlight of this camera is its very low distortion (<3%) of the lens. Currently, NVIDIA official has released the NVIDIA Jetson Orin series developer board. Based on it, we have also adapted the camera into NVIDIA Jetson Orin kernel. The lens on this camera is not interchangeable, if you need to replace the lens, please contact sales in advance! ★ KERNEL CAMERA DRIVER USED This camera relies on kernel drivers to work – one kernel version, one camera driver. Note on supported Nvidia Jetson Boards and Jetpack versions, please refer to Supported JetPack Version for Different NVIDIA Jetson Platforms to ensure compatibility. ★ SUPPORTED PLATFORM DISCLAIMER This camera is specifically designed for the official NVIDIA® Jetson™ Developer Kit by default and does not support third-party carrier boards.
