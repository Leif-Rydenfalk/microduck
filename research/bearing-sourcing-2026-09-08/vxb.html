6700 Deep Groove Ball Bearing - Slim Profile
✔️ 10mm ID - 15mm OD - 3mm Wide - Open Type

The 6700 is a single-row deep groove ball bearing with an ultra-thin 3mm profile, designed for space-constrained, low-friction applications. Made from chrome steel and built for precision, it's ideal for use in robotics, electric motors, compact gearboxes, and lightweight mechanisms.

✅ Type: Deep groove radial ball bearing
✅ Inner diameter: 10mm (0.394")
✅ Outer diameter: 15mm (0.591")
✅ Width: 3mm (0.118")
✅ Material: Chrome steel
✅ Dynamic load rating: 0.85 kN
✅ Static load rating: 0.44 kN
✅ Quantity: 1 bearing
✅ Brand: VXB

💡 Why choose a slim-profile bearing like the 6700?
Its narrow width and lightweight design make it ideal for precision devices where space and weight are critical. The open design also enables efficient lubrication and minimal rotational resistance in clean environments.

For high-speed, compact setups, the 6700 bearing offers dependable performance in a tiny footprint - grab one and keep your designs rolling light and smooth!
