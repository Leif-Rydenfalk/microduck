Overview

The 3525 Waterproof Cavity Speaker is a compact and powerful 3W audio driver designed for embedded systems, portable electronics, and waterproof applications.

With a rated impedance of 8Ω and high-frequency response up to 20kHz, this speaker delivers clear and crisp sound in a durable ABS housing. Its compact dimensions and pre-attached 150mm cable with 1.25mm pitch connector make it easy to integrate into various enclosures and devices.
