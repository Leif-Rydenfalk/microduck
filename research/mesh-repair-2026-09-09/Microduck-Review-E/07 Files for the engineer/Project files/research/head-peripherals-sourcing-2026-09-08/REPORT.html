What closes identity

  • Read original speaker label and measure enclosure including tabs/connector; obtain impedance curve/DC resistance, rated power/SPL test conditions and polarity
  • Verify actual amplifier path and gain. Public PAM8406D is evidence only for public C1; no conclusion about original driver impedance
  • Do not convert 35 × 25 × 7 placeholder into cavity fit approval or assert stock speaker swap from appearance
  • Controller marking, board revision, host bus/address and count; two antennas do not necessarily mean two reader ICs
  • Antenna outer shape,turns,inductance,Q,ferrite,connector,orientation and matching components; measure in assembled head/beak near batteries and metal
  • Full topology and required tag/read/write/card-emulation behavior before choosing PN7150 versus ST25R3916; no cheapest-IC substitution
  • Whether dedicated indicator PCB exists; number/type/colour of emitters, footprint, height, viewing angle, current, driver GPIO and lightpipe
  • Separate passive coloured eye ring from illumination; do not choose RGB ring by appearance
  • Quote 1/100/1000 bench pieces only after configuration choice; full production quantities unknown
