    Product Description

    6700 Radial Ball Bearing

    Key Features

    6700 is a Thin High Speed Ball Bearing manufactured to be Open on both sides and is pre-lubricated with Light Oil allowing it to be ready for use upon delivery. Our Chrome Steel 6700 Series Thin High Speed Ball Bearings are excellent in light radial thrust or combined load applications that require high speeds while taking up as little space as possible.

    Applications / Industries

    6700 Thin High Speed Ball Bearings are used in Scanning Equipment, Navigation tools, Pressure-Regulating Systems, Antenna and Sun Protection Systems, Robotics, Speed Reducers, Bicycles, Optical Equipment, Slip Ring Assemblies, among others.
