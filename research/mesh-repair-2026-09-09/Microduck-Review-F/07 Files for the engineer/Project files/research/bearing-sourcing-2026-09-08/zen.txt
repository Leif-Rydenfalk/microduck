Download Catalogue What are thin section bearings? Thin section bearings are a high-precision family of bearings whose cross-section remains the same as the bore diameter increases. A bearing is considered a thin section bearing when the bore diameter is greater than four times the radial cross-section. Manufactured under German DIN standards Low friction, accuracy, and high efficiency Durability and reliability assured Unique Characteristics These bearings are also known as slim bearings or slim section bearings due to their narrow design that allows them to fit into very tight spaces where conventional bearings might not be suitable. Also, thanks to their thin cross-section, they are lighter than standard bearings, making them perfect for applications where weight is critical. But that is not all! Thin section bearings are designed for high precision accuracy and to operate at high speeds. This makes them suitable for applications where precise positioning and smooth operation are essential and for applications where rapid motion is required. Read More These slim bearing's reduced radial and axial dimensions enable them to handle radial and axial loads while maintaining excellent running accuracy. Furthermore, their reduced torque and heat generation enhance operational performance and reliability. Such characteristics make them perfect for robotics, medical, optical equipment, textile machinery, renewable energy, and marine/subsea applications. They offer options for open, shielded, or sealed versions with standard PA6.6 or brass cages.
