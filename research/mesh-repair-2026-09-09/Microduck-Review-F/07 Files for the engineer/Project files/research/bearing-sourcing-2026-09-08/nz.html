16x22x4 mm (MR1622-ZZ) Bearing


NZD $5.90 +GST each pcs

In Stock Now




SKU: 00206030

This 16 x 22 x 4 mm (MR1622-ZZ) bearing is a metric deep groove ball bearing. The MR1622-ZZ inner diameter is 16 mm, the MR1622-ZZ outer diameter is 22 mm, the MR1622-ZZ width is 4 mm.

This bearing has non-contact metal shields on both sides to protect the bearing from dust or any possible contamination, which can extend bearing life, and retain the lubricant in the bearing.

Specs:

Bearing Model: MR1622-ZZ
Also Known As: ET2216ZZ, DDA2216, DDA2216ZZ, MR1622ZZ
Inner Diameter (d): 16 mm
Outer Diameter (D): 22 mm
Width (B): 4 mm
Material: GCR15 Bearing Steel
Closures: Metal Shielded Both Sides
