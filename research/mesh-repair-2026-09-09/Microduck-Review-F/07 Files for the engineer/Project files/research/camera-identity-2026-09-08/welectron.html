This Arducam IMX219 camera module is mainly designed for NVIDIA® Jetson Nano. The highlight of this camera is its very low distortion (<3%) of the lens.

NOTE: This camera is released for official Jetson Nano/Xavier NX boards. It can also be used on the Raspberry Pi Compute Module (like CM3, CM3+), but it does not support the standard Raspberry Pi models (like Raspberry Pi 3/3B+/4) and other third-party boards.

  • Shutter Type: Rolling Shutter
  • Color Type: Color
  • IR Sensitivity: Visible light, Integral IR Filter
  • Interface: MIPI
  • Format: RAW Bayer
  • Platform Support: Jetson Nano
  • Sensor type: IMX219
  • Optical Size: 1/4 inch
  • Lens spec: EFL: 2.8mm, F.NO: 2.8, View Angle: 75 Deg. (Horizontal)
  • Frame Rate: 21fps@8MP, 30fps@1080p, 120fps@720p
  • Interface Type: 2-Lane MIPI
  • Output format: RAW10
  • Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs)
  • Building Drones
  • Autonomous Robotic Systems
  • Mobile Medical Imaging
  • intelligent Video Analytics (IVA)
