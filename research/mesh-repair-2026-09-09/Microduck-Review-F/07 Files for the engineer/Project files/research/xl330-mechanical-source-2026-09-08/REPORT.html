Frame and depth discrepancy

Local horn axis is X; disc outer faces are X±14.5. Pilot centers are (Y,Z)=(0,±6),(±6,0). The modeled6 mm cut reaches X±8.5, while the documented3 mm maximum reaches X±11.5. The 0.01 mm Boolean overrun in the script is not a manufacturing tolerance. Corner axes atY±8 andZ7.5/−22.5 match the16×30 grid, but straightØ2 through23 mm does not represent manufacturer Detail A/B steppedØ2/Ø1.6 geometry. The 3.5/4.5 callouts are axial dimensions of the shown steps; do not reinterpret them as an approved arbitrary screw insertion depth.

What the tapping note resolves

The manufacturer explicitly calls for M2 tapping screws at the depicted interfaces. A 1.6 mm mesh pilot alone did not establish that. Exact tapping pitch/profile, screw head and length, material, tolerances, torque, actual usable engagement and original per-joint Microduck schedule remain unproven. ISO4762 M2×0.4 is not automatically equivalent. The drawing’s3 mm maximum is a design constraint; it is not a completed physical thread or strength test.
