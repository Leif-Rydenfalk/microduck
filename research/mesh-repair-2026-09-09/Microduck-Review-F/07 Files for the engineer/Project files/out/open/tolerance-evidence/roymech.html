Deep Groove Ball Bearing Standard Designations (ISO)

Deep groove ball bearing dimensions and designation systems comply with ISO 15 rolling bearing boundary dimension standards and ISO 5753 internal clearance classifications.

Bearing Numbering System Overview

Typical rolling bearing designation structure showing the order of the main codes and suffixes. (Note: exact prefix/suffix sets vary by manufacturer.)

Meaning: deep groove ball bearing, 62 series, 25 mm bore, sealed both sides, C3 clearance.

Rule of thumb: the basic designation is usually enough to select size; suffixes refine the bearing’s features for speed, contamination, temperature, and mounting fits.

Bearing designation format:

[Type][Series][Bore] + [Suffixes]

Deep groove ball bearings are identified by a leading 6. The following tables show ISO dimension series, bore codes, and common suffix designations.

Series Example Description Typical Applications
