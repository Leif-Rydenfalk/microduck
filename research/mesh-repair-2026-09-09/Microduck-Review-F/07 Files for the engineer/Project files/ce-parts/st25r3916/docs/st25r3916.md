Why this one and not only the PN7150: the ST25R3916 is a raw AFE + framer
driven register-by-register from a host (the Radxa, over I2C3 or SPI), and
its sheet states two single-ended antenna outputs; the PN7150 is a
firmware-integrated NCI controller with one antenna path. Either could sit
on the HAT; neither is known to.

Standards the sheet claims (vendor claim, not verified here — recorded as a
`standard` ledger row with that caveat): ISO/IEC 14443 A/B, ISO/IEC 15693,
ISO/IEC 18092, EMVCo 3.0 — `ISO14443 and ISO15693 compliant general purpose
NFC device`, `EMVCo 3.0 analog and digital compliant`.
