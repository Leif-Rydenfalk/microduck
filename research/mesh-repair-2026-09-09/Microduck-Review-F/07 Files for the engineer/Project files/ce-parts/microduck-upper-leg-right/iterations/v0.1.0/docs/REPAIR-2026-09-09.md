# Continuous thigh return, v0.1.0

The old constant-offset cavity broke through the rounded outer return. The new
cavity follows the inner radius and uses explicit vertex correspondence between
profiles, avoiding automatic loft twists. The original outer envelope, two servo
axes and connector locations are retained. Right is derived by mirroring left.

Independent STEP readback: six radial wall probes per side measure
1.0099–1.0122 mm of material; all midpoint probes lie in material. The same
midpoint check fails on the previous solid at 55 degrees. Each exported STL is
closed, with zero detected self-intersections, and the CAD is one valid solid.

This fixes the visible split and mesh defects. Physical strength, print adhesion,
whole-assembly interference and fastener retention are not established by these
checks. The nominal wall remains 1 mm; this is not a claim of a load rating.

Evidence: evidence/geometry-2026-09-09/checks.json and the exact STEP/STL files.
