Documented target and gaps

tof: Pinned Pollen runtime supports both L5CX and L8CX; majority L5 per source comment, not per-unit identity. Four-pin J5: GND, 3V3, SDA, SCL. 0x29 is 7-bit; 0x52 is the shifted 8-bit address in supplier tables. Local ToF site is a pose, not a measured module envelope. No module mounting/clearance PASS possible from it.

battery: Reference np_f970 mesh is 38.6 x 20.6 x 70.8 mm. Product calls pack removable NP-F550, 2600 mAh. Similar outer dimensions do not establish contact geometry, latch, polarity, current rating or original identity. Runtime 6.6/8.2 V mapping is an indication algorithm, not pack protection or charger cutoff specification.

charger: docs/BOM.md B5 requires one dual charger; spec/sourcing.json folds charger share into B4 with no separate B5. Whether one charger per robot or shared charging is intended must be explicit.

Source corrections for review

  • B4 PASS sourcing verdict is not original identity, continuous-current or contact-fit PASS. Preserve that distinction in rendering.
  • B4 current unit prices divide indivisible two-pack sets: a single prototype requires a whole 49.90 USD NextBatteries or 49.99 USD Neewer set. One charger per robot requires N sets unless separately sourced, producing 2N packs; ceil(N/2) sets meets pack count but not one-charger-per-robot count.
  • B9 bare optical LGA packages require a designed PCB/power/interface/optical placement. Moving them to the HAT is a design change, not automatically the correct part at 100 robots or a 1:1 substitute.
  • B9 ST SATEL set contains two boards. Do not compare package price directly against one carrier. Their header/flying-wire interface is not the existing Qwiic harness.
  • Generic requirement for regulation/level shifting on every L5 carrier is too broad; SparkFun schematic demonstrates direct 3.3 V L5. Pollen runtime compatibility does not prove identical carrier circuitry.

Quote questions

  • Original pack/charger/ToF markings, revision photographs and dimensioned drawings; ToF height, mounting, optical axis/window and connector exit
  • Pack contact/latch drawings and polarity, cell maker/model, rated continuous and pulse current with temperature/time limits, protection trip/recovery thresholds and required certification records
  • Measured robot load profile required to compare ratings; no conclusion from capacity or aggregate servo count alone
  • Charger input connector/protocol and adapter inclusion, both-channel current under simultaneous charge, voltage tolerance/termination, temperature/protection limits and exact controller identity
  • Quote one prototype then 100/1000 robots with explicit battery and charger quantities, stock date, MOQ, lead time, freight/tax to confirmed destination; no commitment
