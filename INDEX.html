Tools — how every document above is regenerated

Nothing in this repository is hand-maintained if a script can own it. Each tool below regenerates its document from the measured data, so a stale number is a bug with a fix rather than an edit.

ScriptWhat it generatesState
tools/gen_comparison.pyBuilds COMPARISON.html from the measured data.present
tools/gen_index.pyBuilds this index, checking every link.present
tools/gen_test_plan.pyBuilds TEST-PLAN.html from spec/test-plan.json; computes every derived figure (degrees, encoder counts, tilt thresholds) rather than carrying it as text.present
tools/gen_sourcing.pyBuilds SOURCING.html and RFQ.html from spec/sourcing.json; refuses to publish a verdict the data does not support.present
tools/head_analysis.pyMeasures head conformance from silhouettes.present
tools/gen_head_motion.pyBuilds HEAD-MOTION.html from out/motion/head.json.present
sim/head_sweep.pyDrives and measures the four head joints, renders the head videos, probes the collision-free range.present
sim/head_real_video.pyMeasures the real robot's head motion off a product video.present
sim/head_compare.pyPuts our head beside the real one at the same camera.present
sim/head_verify.pyReads every head video back off disk and refuses blank or frozen frames.present
tools/md2html.pyConverts the Markdown docs to the shared HTML style.present
sim/mech_dims.pyMeasures every mesh through the FreeCAD kernel.present
sim/stress_matrix.pyThe structural FEA matrix: parts × load cases × materials.present
sim/compare_render.pyPhoto-matched studio renders and joint close-ups.present
sim/assembly_steps_mj.pyCumulative assembly-step renders.present
sim/run_policy.pyRuns Pollen's trained policies on our meshes.present
sim/gait_sweep.pyThe gait-robustness matrix: speed, mass, friction, slope and push, with per-joint torque and per-foot ground reaction at 200 Hz.present
sim/gait_evidence.pyAggregates the sweep into out/sim-evidence/gait-peaks.json and gait-robustness.json.present
sim/battery_runtime.pyIntegrates per-servo electrical power over the measured gait into hours on the pack.present
electronics/gen_ee.pyGenerates the block diagram, schematics and layout.present

Run anything under sim/ or that imports the CAD kernel with ce-cad/bin/cad <script.py> — the system python3 has neither numpy nor PIL.
